Privacy Policy

MVP / demo software. Agile Comrade is an MVP decision-support tool for Scrum Masters and Delivery Managers: it surfaces potential delivery risks early so teams can investigate and act sooner. AI results are signals, not guarantees — they may contain false positives, miss risks, or lack context.

Last updated: October 2026

1. What data we keep — and where it lives

Your profile lives in Supabase and contains:

  • Connection profile: profile slug, Jira Cloud URL, Jira email, project keys, and AI provider/model preferences.
  • Cached sprint snapshot: issue keys, summaries, truncated descriptions (~500 characters), statuses, story points, assignee names, labels, priority, due dates, sprint dates, burndown history, scope baselines (issue key → points), and velocity/delivery-health forecasts.
  • Your risk decisions: the status, note, and owner you assign to each risk.
  • Cached AI analyses: prompts and generated mitigation plans, kept with the snapshot until regenerated or deleted.

Your Jira API token and LLM API key are encrypted at rest with Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256) and used only on the server, never returned to your browser. Your dashboard access token stays in your browser; the server keeps only its SHA-256 hash.

Your browser also keeps an offline copy of your latest dashboard data, plus a few small preferences.

Deleting your profile in Settings removes all of the above, including local browser copies. Data sent to AI/LLM when you want to leverage AI remains under that provider's policy (see section 4).

2. Data sent to AI/LLM when you want to leverage AI

Nothing leaves your server until you click an AI feature — analysis is never automatic.

When you trigger an analysis we send relevant Jira data: issue keys, summaries (truncated to ~300 characters), descriptions and acceptance criteria (truncated to ~250 characters), statuses, story points, due dates, sprint info, aggregated sprint metrics (burndown gaps), and — for current-sprint analysis — your saved risk decisions. Up to ~30 issues are included per analysis. The snapshot you view in the app keeps fuller context (~500-character stored descriptions); this trimming applies only to what leaves the server.

  • Assignees are pseudonymized freshly for each request (John Smith → dev-01).
  • Email addresses are scrubbed before analysis.

For sprint and follow-up analyses this scrubbing is applied to the full payload. Review AI-generated free-text outputs before sharing externally — names or other sensitive details may still appear inside summaries/descriptions (pseudonymization is not anonymization).

Your provider controls your data — this app can't override that. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use submitted prompts for product improvement — use a paid-tier key if that's unacceptable. For OpenRouter, prefer providers with a no-training / zero-retention policy. Third-party AI providers each have their own data-use, retention and training policies that vary by service, model and plan.

Pseudonymization is not anonymization. We replace assignee names with fresh per-call pseudonyms (John Smith → dev-01) and strip emails before anything leaves your server, but names, customer information or other sensitive details can still appear inside free text (summaries/descriptions) and reach your provider. Review your provider's current terms before enabling AI analysis — and if your organization forbids sending Jira data to external AI services, leave AI analysis off for that workspace.

3. What we don't do

  • We never write to your Jira workspace — access is strictly read-only. We never create, modify, transition or delete issues.
  • AI-generated follow-up messages are drafts for you to review and copy & paste manually.
  • We don't send real assignee names as identifiers, don't send email addresses, don't sell data, and keep no analytics trackers.
  • We request only the Jira permissions required to read the configured sprint/issue data.

4. Retention & your controls

  • Data is retained only while your profile exists — cached snapshots are rebuilt on each sync and deleted with the profile (see section 1 for what's stored where, and how removing your profile clears it).
  • AI analysis runs only when you explicitly trigger it. Results are returned to your browser and cached with the snapshot until the next snapshot refresh.
  • Deleting your profile doesn't retract data already submitted to a third-party AI provider, which remains subject to that provider's retention policies.
